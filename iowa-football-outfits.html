Build a Game-Day Outfit

Deciding what to wear to an Iowa football game can be simple. Start with a comfortable base, add black and gold accents, and choose layers that fit the weather and your plans.

A classic game-day outfit might include jeans, a black top, a gold sweatshirt, and comfortable sneakers. If you prefer a sporty look, try a team-inspired jersey over a long-sleeve shirt with denim and a baseball cap.

Three Outfit Ideas to Try

1. Casual and Classic

Pair straight-leg jeans with a black tee, a gold sweatshirt, and sneakers. This combination is easy to wear from a tailgate to the stands.

2. Sporty and Relaxed

Style an oversized jersey with leggings or relaxed denim. Add a cap, crew socks, and supportive shoes for a laid-back look.

3. Polished Gameday Style

Try a black skirt with a fitted sweater, a denim jacket, and boots suitable for the day's conditions. Gold jewelry can bring the colors together.

What Should You Bring?

  • A layer you can add or remove as temperatures change.
  • Comfortable shoes for walking and standing.
  • A small bag for essentials, following the venue's current bag rules.
  • Sunglasses or weather-appropriate accessories.

Plan for the Whole Day

Think about how long you will be outside, whether you will be walking between locations, and what the forecast looks like. Choose an outfit that feels comfortable for your plans instead of focusing on appearance alone.
