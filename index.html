Iowa Gameday Outfits: Find Your Look

Game day is about more than the final score. It is a chance to meet up with friends, enjoy a tailgate, and show your Hawkeye spirit. The Iowa Gameday Edit is a fictional fashion guide with outfit inspiration for every kind of football Saturday.

Whether you love denim and sneakers, an oversized jersey, or a polished black-and-gold outfit, start here to plan a look that feels like you.

Three Tips for a Great Game-Day Look

  1. Start with a color palette. Black, gold, white, and denim are easy to combine.
  2. Dress for your plans. Consider whether you will be tailgating outdoors, walking across campus, or sitting in the stands.
  3. Make it personal. Add a favorite hat, simple jewelry, a team-inspired layer, or a bag that keeps your essentials close.
