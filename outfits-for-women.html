Iowa Football Outfits for Women

Three ways to bring your own style to game day.

Find Your Game-Day Style

Iowa football outfits for women can be sporty, casual, trendy, or dressed up. The easiest way to build a look is to choose one piece you love, then coordinate the rest of your outfit around it.

Black and gold are classic choices, but denim, white basics, neutral knits, and simple accessories can make your outfit feel more personal.

Casual and Comfortable

Try straight-leg jeans, a fitted black top, a gold sweatshirt, and sneakers. A baseball cap and small crossbody bag add practical finishing touches. This look works well for a relaxed tailgate or a full day on campus.

Sporty and Trendy

Style an oversized jersey with leggings, biker shorts in warmer weather, or relaxed denim. Add crew socks and sneakers for a coordinated finish. Layer a long-sleeve shirt underneath when the temperature drops.

Dressed-Up Gameday

For a more elevated look, pair a black skirt with a gold knit top, or wear dark denim with a fitted sweater and boots. Gold-toned jewelry, a belt, or a structured jacket can bring the outfit together without making it feel overdone.

Accessories That Finish the Look

  • Gold-toned earrings or a simple necklace.
  • A black or gold cap, headband, or scarf.
  • A crossbody bag for everyday essentials.
  • A jacket or extra layer chosen for the forecast.

The best outfit is one you feel comfortable wearing throughout the day. For more ideas, visit our game-day outfit guide or plan ahead with our cold-weather styling tips.
